# WiDS 6.0 — Limit Order Book in Python

Build an educational matching engine, measure its performance and investigate short-term price predictability in simulated markets.

Four weeks, 6–8 hours/week, 4–6 participants subject to club allocation. Individual implementations and research throughout. Guided hybrid format with weekly teaching, doubt-clearing and feedback.

## Weekly flow
| Week | Work | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price-time priority, limit/market, cancel, depth, Python design | 3/3 Foundation traces, design notes |
| 2 | IOC, FOK, passive modify, five independent edge tests | 7/7 cumulative, updated design |
| 3 | Basic repeatable measurements or Intermediate profiling, complexity and one optimisation | Results, interpretation, correctness retained |
| 4 | Agent-based simulator, control/signal datasets, EDA, features, logistic regression and baselines | Individual notebook and concise research summary |
| Final | Reproducible individual submission | Repository, documentation and completion review |

The educational specification is independent of the C++ inspiration. Participants choose their own internal data structures. Cancel Newest STP is optional; advanced ML models are optional. Both benchmark tracks are equally valid.

## Selection
10-minute screening quiz covering basic Python, simple problem-solving, data structures and motivation. No finance or ML prerequisites.

## Deliverables
Individual repository, source, design notes, tests, benchmark results and explanation, reproducible research notebook, and a concise project report or detailed README. See assignments/ and [completion criteria](RUBRIC.md).
