Optional Cancel Newest bonus up to 5 (3 for correct matching/cancellation, 2 for FOK atomicity and explanation); final capped at 100. Basic and Intermediate benchmarking can both earn 15/15; no raw speed or predictive-accuracy ranking. Honest null results eligible for full marks. Award correctness points proportionately to demonstrated requirements including mentor's independent tests; do not award points for editing supplied fixtures. Individual authorship and reproducibility confirmed by viva.

## Cohort leaderboard
Update after weekly checkpoints. Use participant IDs/aliases, not names in public locations. Rank final capped score descending, then mandatory correctness subtotal, then research subtotal; exact ties share rank. Provisional scores are marked as such, ungraded values blank; privately share feedback. Do not publish personal grades in GitHub. Template: templates/leaderboard.csv. This is a proposed operational tie-break rule for the confirmed competitive assessment.
